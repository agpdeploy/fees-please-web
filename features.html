Availability Hub

Players don’t need the app

Your players can do everything they need without installing the app or registering an account. They can log their availability and see who else is playing.

All you have to do is post your Availability Hub link in the group chat. Players tap it to update their availability in any phone browser.

  • One tap to log status. Players can tell you if they’re in, out or a maybe in a single tap
  • See who else is playing. Players can see the live availability status of the rest of the roster
  • Get privacy by default. The app hides player surnames by default on public team links. You only see the first letter of their surname. For example - ‘John C’
  • Players can sign up to get their own dashboard. There’s an optional email login for players who want to see their personal match history and balance. They still don’t need the app!
